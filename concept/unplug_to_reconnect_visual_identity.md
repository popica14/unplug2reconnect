# UNPLUG TO RECONNECT — VISUAL IDENTITY

## Brand Idea
**One week offline. One week of real life.**  
A contemporary analogue outdoor identity for a one-week experience in the Romanian Carpathians, built around **digital reset, confidence, real human connection, practical activity and nature**.

**Core philosophy:**  
### LESS DIGITAL. MORE ANALOGUE.

**Brand character:** raw · warm · outdoors · youthful · intelligent · human · tactile · imperfect · contemporary

**Avoid:** luxury glamping · corporate wellness · childish summer-camp aesthetics · survivalist/military styling · generic eco-branding · anti-tech messaging

---

## Visual Concept — SIGNAL / NO SIGNAL
The identity combines fragments of the **digital world** with the physical reality of the outdoors.

Use subtle references to:
- signal bars
- battery indicators
- timestamps
- coordinates
- system/monospace typography
- switches and status labels

Pair them with:
- mountains
- maps
- handwriting
- documentary photography
- paper
- canvas
- timber
- fire
- mud
- practical objects

The visual idea is simple: **we came from digital life — now we are here.**

## Logo Direction
### Primary Wordmark
**UNPLUG**  
`to`  
**R E C O N N E C T**

Use a bold condensed sans serif. Keep **UNPLUG** compact and strong; allow **RECONNECT** to feel more open.

### Symbol
A minimal icon combining **mobile signal bars + a mountain silhouette**.

Avoid crossed-out phones, crossed-out Wi-Fi symbols, trees or obvious “digital detox” clichés.

### Secondary Graphic Device
`DIGITAL ○────● ANALOGUE`

Can be adapted:
- `SCROLLING ○────● LIVING`
- `WATCHING ○────● DOING`
- `ONLINE ○────● OUTSIDE`

## Typography

### Primary — Bold Condensed Sans
For logo, headlines, posters, signage and clothing.

Suggested: **Barlow Condensed / Archivo Narrow / Roboto Condensed / League Gothic**

Example:

> ONE WEEK  
> OFFLINE.  
> ONE WEEK  
> OF REAL LIFE.

### Secondary — Clean Humanist Sans
For body copy and practical information.

Suggested: **Inter / IBM Plex Sans / Source Sans 3**

### Accent — Monospace
For timestamps, coordinates, labels and digital references.

Suggested: **IBM Plex Mono / Space Mono**

Example:  
`DAY 04 / 07:30 / CARPATHIANS / RO`

## Photography
**Documentary, not promotional.**

Photograph the experience rather than staged groups:
dirty hands · wet boots · cooking · backpacks · maps · laughter · smoke · campfires · cards · tents · mud · morning fog · practical work · unplanned moments

Allow imperfection:
- grain
- flash
- motion blur
- crooked framing
- overexposure
- timestamps
- participant-shot images

Participant camera photography should eventually become part of the brand itself.

---

## Graphic Language
Use a structured editorial grid interrupted by analogue elements.

Core devices:
- topographic lines
- real coordinates
- handwritten annotations
- arrows
- stamps
- route marks
- checkboxes
- timestamps
- equipment labels

Examples:

`FIELD TEST 001`  
`DAY 03`  
`NO SIGNAL`  
`10–12 PEOPLE`  
`CARPATHIANS / RO`

## Tone of Voice
Short. Clear. Intelligent. Occasionally dry or playful. Never preachy or overly inspirational.

### Core Messages
**UNPLUG TO RECONNECT**

**ONE WEEK OFFLINE.  
LESS DIGITAL.  
MORE ANALOGUE.**

### Supporting Lines
- Your phone will survive a week without you.
- No Wi-Fi. Good company.
- You may experience boredom.
- Bring boots. Leave your feed behind.
- 10 people. 7 days. 0 algorithms deciding what comes next.
- Capture it. Don’t post it.
- Go outside.

---

## Overall Creative Direction
**Field manual × independent magazine × mountain expedition × youth culture**

The identity should feel like something participants **use, wear, carry and remember** — not decoration added on top of the experience.
